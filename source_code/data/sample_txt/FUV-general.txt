Fulbright University Vietnam is a private, nonprofit university located in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Established in 2016, the university was founded with the goal of creating a new model of higher education in Vietnam based on academic excellence, intellectual freedom, interdisciplinary learning, and public service. Since welcoming its first undergraduate cohort in 2018, Fulbright has grown steadily and has become one of the most recognized liberal arts institutions in Southeast Asia. As of Academic Year 2024-2025, the university enrolled 832 students, including 716 undergraduate students and 116 graduate students. Students came from all regions of Vietnam, representing more than 50 provinces and cities.

The origins of Fulbright University Vietnam can be traced back to the Fulbright Economics Teaching Program established in 1994. This program later evolved into the Fulbright School of Public Policy and Management and educated thousands of policymakers, economists, civil servants, and business leaders over three decades. In 2012, discussions began regarding the establishment of an independent university based on the liberal arts model. In 2014, the United States Congress approved funding to support the initiative. In May 2016, the university was officially announced during a visit by U.S. President Barack Obama to Vietnam. The institution received its operating license in 2017 and admitted its first undergraduate class in 2018 with approximately 100 students.

Between 2018 and 2024, student enrollment increased steadily. Undergraduate enrollment grew from approximately 100 students in 2018 to 245 students in 2020, 421 students in 2022, 612 students in 2023, and 716 students in 2024. Graduate enrollment reached 116 students in 2024. During the same period, the university expanded its faculty, academic programs, student services, and campus infrastructure. The student population maintained a nearly balanced gender distribution, with approximately 51 percent female students and 49 percent male students during Academic Year 2024-2025.

One of the defining characteristics of Fulbright University Vietnam is its commitment to liberal arts and sciences education. Unlike traditional university systems where students must immediately select a specialized field of study, Fulbright encourages exploration across multiple disciplines before students formally declare a major. During their first two years, students complete a common curriculum that introduces them to various methods of inquiry, analytical approaches, and intellectual traditions. This educational philosophy reflects the belief that future leaders must understand complex issues from multiple perspectives rather than through a single disciplinary lens.

The undergraduate curriculum is structured around a combination of foundational courses, major requirements, elective courses, and experiential learning opportunities. During Academic Year 2024-2025, Fulbright offered more than 220 undergraduate courses across different academic fields. Students completed an average of 32 courses before graduation, equivalent to approximately 128 academic credits. The curriculum emphasized critical thinking, quantitative reasoning, communication, creativity, teamwork, ethical reasoning, and problem solving.

Students at Fulbright could choose from 11 undergraduate majors. These majors included Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Integrated Science, Human Centered Engineering, Economics, Psychology, Social Studies, History, Literature, Vietnam Studies, and Art and Media Studies. Among these programs, Economics remained the largest major with approximately 18 percent of undergraduate students enrolled. Computer Science accounted for 15 percent of students, Psychology represented 13 percent, and Human Centered Engineering represented 10 percent. The remaining students were distributed across the other academic disciplines.

The university also encouraged interdisciplinary study through a series of academic minors. During 2024, students could pursue minors in Business, Policy and Society, East Asian Studies, Gender and Sexuality Studies, and Science and Technology Studies. Approximately 38 percent of undergraduate students completed at least one minor in addition to their major. Around 9 percent of students pursued double majors, demonstrating the flexibility of the curriculum and the university’s emphasis on intellectual exploration.

Classroom learning at Fulbright differs significantly from the traditional lecture based model commonly found in many universities. Most courses are discussion based and emphasize active participation. Advanced courses are capped at 20 students, while introductory courses generally enroll between 15 and 35 students. The average class size during Academic Year 2024-2025 was approximately 18 students. More than 78 percent of all undergraduate courses enrolled fewer than 25 students. This structure allows students to engage directly with faculty members and participate actively in classroom discussions.

Faculty quality represents another important aspect of the Fulbright educational model. In 2024, the university employed approximately 72 full time faculty members and more than 30 visiting faculty members. About 96 percent of full time faculty members held doctoral degrees from universities in North America, Europe, Australia, or leading Asian institutions. Faculty members represented more than 20 nationalities and brought expertise from disciplines ranging from artificial intelligence and engineering to literature and public policy.

The student to faculty ratio was approximately 11 to 1 during Academic Year 2024-2025. This relatively low ratio allowed faculty members to provide individualized mentoring and academic support. According to university surveys conducted in 2024, more than 87 percent of students reported meeting faculty members outside class at least once per month for academic advising, research supervision, or career guidance.

Research plays an increasingly important role in the undergraduate experience. During 2024, students participated in more than 90 faculty supervised research projects. These projects covered topics such as climate change adaptation, urban development, artificial intelligence, Vietnamese literature, entrepreneurship, public health, renewable energy, and educational innovation. Approximately 43 percent of graduating students participated in at least one formal research project before completing their degree.

Experiential learning constitutes another key pillar of the Fulbright curriculum. The university requires students to engage with real world challenges through internships, community projects, research experiences, or entrepreneurial initiatives. During Summer 2024, more than 280 undergraduate students completed internships at over 170 organizations. These organizations included technology companies, nonprofit organizations, government agencies, consulting firms, educational institutions, and multinational corporations.

Community engagement remains central to the Fulbright mission. In Academic Year 2024-2025, students collectively contributed more than 12,000 hours of community service through educational programs, environmental initiatives, social impact projects, and nonprofit partnerships. More than 65 student organizations operated on campus, covering interests such as entrepreneurship, technology, debate, music, theater, sports, sustainability, and social impact.

Financial accessibility is another defining feature of the institution. During Academic Year 2024-2025, more than 60 percent of students received partial or full financial aid. Approximately 22 percent of students received scholarships covering at least 75 percent of tuition expenses. The university allocated several million dollars annually to scholarships and financial assistance programs. These initiatives were designed to ensure that talented students from diverse socioeconomic backgrounds could access high quality education regardless of financial circumstances.

Technology plays an increasingly important role within the academic environment. During 2024, Fulbright maintained more than 10 specialized laboratories supporting teaching and research activities. These facilities included computer laboratories, engineering design spaces, multimedia studios, collaborative learning spaces, and scientific research facilities. Computer Science students had access to high performance computing resources supporting coursework in machine learning, data science, and software engineering.

The university’s permanent campus is located within Saigon High Tech Park in Thu Duc City. The site occupies approximately 15 hectares and has been designed to support future expansion. Campus planning includes academic buildings, research facilities, student residences, athletic facilities, innovation spaces, and community gathering areas. The long term development plan aims to accommodate several thousand students while maintaining the close knit academic culture that characterizes the institution today.

International engagement is also an important dimension of student life. In 2024, Fulbright maintained academic partnerships with more than 30 universities and organizations worldwide. Students participated in exchange programs, collaborative research initiatives, and international conferences. Approximately 18 percent of undergraduate students participated in an international educational experience before graduation.

The university has also embraced emerging technologies and new academic fields. Beginning with the Class of 2029, Fulbright announced plans to introduce additional academic pathways in areas such as Artificial Intelligence and Business, Policy and Society. These new offerings reflect growing student demand and broader labor market trends. According to industry projections analyzed by the university, demand for professionals with expertise in artificial intelligence, data analytics, and interdisciplinary problem solving is expected to increase significantly over the next decade.

Graduate outcomes provide another measure of the university’s impact. According to internal surveys conducted among recent graduates, approximately 91 percent of graduates secured employment or admission to graduate school within six months of graduation. Graduates pursued careers in technology, consulting, education, finance, public policy, entrepreneurship, media, and nonprofit organizations. Others continued their studies at universities in Asia, Europe, Australia, and North America.

Looking toward the future, Fulbright University Vietnam aims to become one of Asia’s leading liberal arts and sciences institutions. The university’s strategic plan includes increasing enrollment, expanding academic programs, strengthening research capacity, developing new partnerships, and enhancing student opportunities. Through its commitment to interdisciplinary education, critical inquiry, public service, and innovation, Fulbright seeks to prepare future generations of leaders capable of addressing the complex challenges facing Vietnam, Southeast Asia, and the wider world. The institution’s emphasis on intellectual breadth, academic rigor, and social responsibility reflects a vision of higher education that extends beyond career preparation and seeks to cultivate thoughtful citizens who can contribute meaningfully to society throughout their lives.
